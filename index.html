EML to HTML converter

Nothing you upload here is sent anywhere. This converter runs entirely in your browser.

Select a saved email file and this pulls out the HTML part, decodes it, and shows you the raw markup, ready to copy into an email testing tool.

Decoded HTML

What this handles

  • Inline images stay as cid: references. They will not resolve, which is fine when you are testing markup rather than rendering the email.
  • Plain-text-only emails produce a message rather than silently handing back the text version.
  • Uncommon character sets fall back to UTF-8, and the page tells you when that happens.
